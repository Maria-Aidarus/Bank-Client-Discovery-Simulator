### What was missed

- Ask about Sharia-compliant requirements and whether payroll dates are fixed; ask for any supplier quote or purchase order for the larger orders before discussing expansion finance.
- If a gap is confirmed, explain that cash management can track overdue invoices and projected balances but does not fund the gap.
